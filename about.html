Who We Are

Tshwaragano Pawnshop is located at Plot 125 Main Mall in Gaborone, Botswana.

We offer pawnshop services involving different types of items, including vehicles, household furniture, jewellery, electronics, tools and equipment.

Our services allow customers to enquire about pawning or selling their valuable items. Customers can contact us for more information about our services and the process involved.
